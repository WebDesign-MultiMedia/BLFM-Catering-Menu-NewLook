SMS Notifications and Specials

Buffet Lucia's Fiesta Mexicana offers two optional kinds of text messages. Order texts cover your food orders, catering services, payment confirmations, receipts, and order status updates. Specials are promotional texts about weekend specials, new menu items, catering availability, and events. Each has its own checkbox below, so you can choose either one, both, or neither.

Order texts

  • Food orders
  • Catering orders
  • Payment confirmations
  • Receipts
  • Order status updates

Specials (up to 4 messages per month)

  • Weekend specials
  • New menu items
  • Catering and party trays
  • Events

SMS enrollment is completely optional. You may place an order, request catering services, make a payment, or receive an email receipt without providing a mobile phone number or agreeing to receive text messages.
