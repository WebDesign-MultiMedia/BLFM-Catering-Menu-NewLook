Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Julio Salas SMS Testing collects, uses, stores, and protects information provided by users who voluntarily enroll in personal software-test SMS notifications.

This personal software-testing program is operated by Julio Salas. The pages are hosted on this website for development and testing purposes.

Information Collected

This program may collect:

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Optional test reference number
  • Date and time of SMS consent
  • Consent-page URL
  • Exact consent disclosure accepted
  • Test food-order information
  • Test catering-request information
  • Test payment or receipt details
  • SMS delivery and opt-out status

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

  • Record and verify affirmative SMS consent
  • Deliver requested transactional software-test messages
  • Test food-order and catering notification functionality
  • Send test payment and receipt confirmations
  • Send application-status notifications
  • Process STOP and HELP requests
  • Maintain necessary technical and compliance records
  • Troubleshoot application delivery problems

Mobile Information Non-Sharing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and messaging consent will not be sold, rented, shared, or otherwise provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Service Providers

Limited information may be processed by technical service providers only when necessary to:

  • Deliver requested SMS messages
  • Host the consent form
  • Store consent records
  • Maintain application security
  • Troubleshoot technical problems

These service providers may not use your mobile information or consent information for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS Disclosures

  • Message frequency varies
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe
  • Reply HELP for assistance
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase
  • Enrollment is voluntary
  • No marketing or promotional messages are sent

See the SMS Consent page and the Terms and Conditions for more information.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Julio Salas at buffetluciasfiestamexicana@gmail.com.
