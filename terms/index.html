Program Name

Buffet Lucia's Fiesta Mexicana SMS Notifications and Specials

Program Description

Buffet Lucia's Fiesta Mexicana SMS Notifications and Specials sends two types of text messages. Each type requires its own separate opt-in checkbox on our SMS sign-up page, and customers may choose either, both, or neither:

  • Order texts (transactional) — messages concerning food orders, catering services, payment confirmations, receipts, catering-event information, and order status updates.
  • Specials (promotional) — messages about weekend specials, menu updates, catering availability, and events.

Message Frequency

Order texts: sent as needed for your food orders and catering services — for example, payment confirmations, receipts, and order status updates. Message frequency varies based on your order activity.

Specials: up to 4 messages per month.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Opt-Out and Help

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Replying STOP stops all text messages from this number, including both order texts and specials. Reply HELP for assistance. After opting out, you may still request receipts or order updates by email or phone.

Carrier Liability

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Consent Is Not a Condition of Purchase

Opting in to this SMS program is entirely voluntary. You are never required to consent to receive text messages in order to place a food order, book catering services, or complete a purchase with Buffet Lucia's Fiesta Mexicana.
