## Campaign description
Buffet Lucia's Fiesta Mexicana, a Mexican restaurant and catering business in the Bronx, NY, uses this campaign to send text messages to customers who have opted in. Messages include (1) order-related texts such as food order and catering confirmations, payment confirmations, receipts, and order status updates, and (2) occasional promotional texts about weekend specials, new menu items, catering and party tray availability, and restaurant events. Customers opt in through our SMS sign-up page, which has two separate checkboxes, both unchecked by default: one for order texts and one for promotional texts. Promotional texts are sent only to customers who check the promotional checkbox. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

## How do end-users consent to receive messages?
End users opt in by visiting our SMS sign-up page at https://buffetluciasfiestamexicana.com/sms-consent/, entering their name and mobile number, and checking one or both of two separate SMS consent checkboxes, each unchecked by default. Checkbox 1 (order texts) reads: "I agree to receive transactional text messages from Buffet Lucia's Fiesta Mexicana regarding my food order, catering service, payment confirmation, receipt, or order status. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Consent is not a condition of purchase." Checkbox 2 (promotional texts) reads: "I agree to receive promotional text messages from Buffet Lucia's Fiesta Mexicana about weekend specials, menu updates, catering availability, and events. Message frequency varies, up to 4 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Consent is not a condition of purchase." Signing up is optional and not required to place an order or make a purchase; users may select "No thanks - continue without SMS." Promotional texts are sent only to users who check the promotional checkbox. The sign-up page is promoted with a QR code displayed in the restaurant.
