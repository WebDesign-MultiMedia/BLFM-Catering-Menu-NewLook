Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Buffet Lucia's Fiesta Mexicana ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you request a catering quote, place a food order, or sign up for our text messages: transactional order texts (about your orders, payments, and receipts) and/or promotional texts about our weekend specials, menu updates, catering availability, and events.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, phone number, and email address when you request a catering quote or place a food order. When you complete a purchase, we also collect payment and receipt details, such as the amount paid and the date of the transaction. If you opt in to SMS order texts, SMS promotional texts (specials), or both, we additionally collect your mobile phone number, which type(s) of texts you chose, the date and time you gave consent, an optional order or catering reference number, and the exact consent disclosure shown to you for each type at sign-up.

Why We Collect This Information

We collect this information to respond to catering inquiries, prepare and fulfill food orders, process payments, generate receipts, and send you the transactional updates you request, such as payment confirmations, receipt confirmations, and order status updates.

Promotional text messages (weekend specials, menu updates, catering availability, and events) are sent only to customers who separately check the specials checkbox on our SMS sign-up page. Your order and catering details, and any consent you give to receive order texts, are never used to send you marketing messages without that separate opt-in.

How We Use Food-Order and Catering Information

Information about your food order or catering event — such as the event date, number of guests, and dishes requested — is used only to prepare your quote, plan your order, and coordinate delivery or pickup. We do not use this information for any purpose unrelated to fulfilling your order.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and messaging consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your information is only shared with the service providers we use to deliver receipts and text messages (such as our email and SMS delivery providers) and to maintain our internal bookkeeping records.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

SMS Program

Customers may voluntarily opt in to text messages from Buffet Lucia's Fiesta Mexicana at our SMS sign-up page. There are two types of messages, and each has its own separate, unchecked opt-in checkbox:

  • Order texts — transactional messages about your food orders, catering services, payment confirmations, receipts, and order status updates. Message frequency varies.
  • Specials — promotional messages about weekend specials, menu updates, catering availability, and events. Up to 4 messages per month.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for assistance. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.
